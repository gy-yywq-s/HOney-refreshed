## Verdict

**REDESIGN** — by the Phase 3 rule (total 16 < 20; no principle at 0). As in r3–r9 the word overstates the scope: tokens, type, spacing, colour, motion budget, weight, service worker, trust architecture and now the idle budget all verify. What the score names is a contract problem, not a style problem: the app ships new surfaces and restyles without carrying the contracts the previous round established (states on every branch, a landing that keeps the heading, one formatter, one truth per channel, an affordance for every action), and it does so mid-audit.

1. #8 + #2 — States and landings for the surfaces that shipped without them (Find mode, the chooser, the `/api/me` shell, offline, History "Select"/chooser landing).
2. #4 + #2 + #8 — The phone Timetable within the owner's decisions: a keyboard/AT and no-touch path to sync, freshness surfaced somewhere a phone student can read it, one Tab stop for the date, the heading that stays, Tab 1 = skip link, the nav clear of the canvas foot, the now-line off the labels.
