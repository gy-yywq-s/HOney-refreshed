# HOney wordmark brainstorm

Exploratory only. None of these files replaces the current product asset.

Hard constraints:

- The full name must read as `HOney`.
- The `One` sequence must remain readable even where the `O` hints at a `C`, campus entrance, courtyard, or route.
- The graphic idea must belong to the lettering rather than sit beside it as a generic logo.
- No bee, honeycomb, graduation cap, map pin, or editorial-luxury treatment.
- Color is exploratory and is not tied to the current app theme tokens.

Directions:

1. **Aperture tile** — an open `O` gains a detached tile at the opening, so it reads as both an entrance and a complete letter.
2. **One route** — the `O`, `n`, and `e` share a single path; the route node is structural, not decorative.
3. **Courtyard** — the `O` is a campus-like courtyard with a visible entry path, while the internal dot prevents a power-button reading.
4. **Open door** — a small hinged door occupies the `O` aperture and becomes part of the letter.
5. **Returning tail** — the `y` terminal returns below the word and closes the open `O`, making the full wordmark one graphic.
6. **Nested One** — `n` and `e` tuck into a large open `O`, giving `One` a secondary emblem-like silhouette without losing letter order.

`imagegen-component-reference.png` is the rejected raw component sheet. It is retained only to document the component-only workflow; its glow, dark background, and literal symbols are not used in the vector concepts.
