# Round 02 — all-letter campus systems

This round replaces the rejected O-only exploration. Every letter participates in one shared graphic grammar, with the `One` cluster doing the heaviest semantic work.

1. **One / cam dual read** — O↔c, n↔a, and e↔m hints are distributed across the three-letter cluster without changing the primary reading.
2. **Campus circulation** — a single route is structurally threaded through H, O, n, e, and y; each glyph becomes a different spatial function.
3. **Courtyard alphabet** — every glyph is built from the same architectural modules: boundary, opening, bridge, turn, and fork.
4. **Two plazas and a bridge** — the O and e are paired places, n is their connector, H is the entry gate, and y is the outgoing fork.
5. **Folded ribbon** — a non-campus direction in which every letter is a different fold of one stroke.
6. **Counter relay** — a non-literal graphic system carried by migrating negative-space shapes.
7. **Hinged letters** — a tool-like joint appears in every glyph, suggesting opening and adaptation rather than a place.
8. **One lockup** — the three middle letters interlock into an extractable graphic while H and y join its motion.

These remain brainstorm drawings, not final production assets.
